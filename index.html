About Me

Designer by passion, developer by craft

আসসালামু আলাইকুম! I'm Muhammad Talhah Maknun, a graphic designer and software developer from Mymensingh, Bangladesh. I founded Tamaddun Design, a small studio focused on logo design, brand identity and Islamic art.

My best-known work is Tamaddun Keyboard — a full-featured Bengali input method for Windows and Android with phonetic typing, Gboard-style layouts, themes, stickers and AI writing tools, used and loved by Bengali typists.

Alongside client design work, I build free, practical web tools for the community — a complete Quran app, a PDF toolbox, a school & madrasa management system, and more. I also lead the তাহযিবুল উম্মাহ online Islamic community.

Design philosophy: clean, meaningful, and useful — every pixel and every line of code should serve a real person.
